Connect or reconnect QuickBooks

This private local utility uses Intuit’s official OAuth 2.0 Playground for its production authorization workflow. This instruction page does not receive authorization codes or tokens.

  1. Launch the installed desktop utility on your own computer.
  2. Configure the application’s production credentials and the official Playground redirect address in your local config.json, following the supplied README.
  3. Open the Intuit OAuth Playground. Select the same application’s Production credentials and Accounting scope.
  4. An authorized company administrator signs in on Intuit’s official pages, selects the company, and grants consent.
  5. In the Playground, click Get Tokens. Copy the company identifier (realmId) and Refresh Token into the local configuration fields realm_id and refresh_token.
  6. In the local utility, expand “连接与配置” and click “载入配置与授权”. The utility refreshes the token, verifies company access, and saves the authorization in the operating system credential store.

Use these same steps when an expired or revoked connection needs reauthorization. Do not place credentials or tokens in this website, a GitHub repository, or a message. After import, do not continue refreshing the same authorization in the Playground, since the desktop utility manages token rotation.

Load configuration only after exporting any working records you need to keep. Read the Privacy Policy for storage and deletion details.
