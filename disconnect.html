Disconnect QuickBooks

Opening this instruction page does not revoke authorization or confirm that a connection has been revoked.

Disconnect from the local utility

  1. Launch the installed desktop utility.
  2. Expand “连接与配置”.
  3. Click “撤销授权并清空记录”. The utility requests revocation from Intuit and, on success, removes its stored authorization tokens and current working records.

If the revocation request fails, retry or use the connected-app management controls in QuickBooks Online to remove the app’s connection. If you arrived here after disconnecting from QuickBooks, revocation is handled by QuickBooks; local cleanup remains a separate step.

Clean up local information

Application configuration may remain in the operating system credential store. To remove all stored application credentials and tokens, remove entries for InvoiceToFedEx-v1 using your operating system’s credential-management tools. Clear any remaining secrets or bootstrap tokens in config.json, and separately delete exported CSV files, diagnostic logs, copies, and backups as needed.

Closing the browser or uninstalling the utility does not automatically revoke the connection or delete exported files. Removing a connection does not delete source records in QuickBooks. See the Privacy Policy for details.
