GetInvoice

A private desktop utility for designated family members and authorized internal users. It reads QuickBooks Online invoices, lets users review shipping information, and exports a CSV file for manual carrier import.

Launch the utility

This site provides instructions and policies; it does not run the desktop utility or host invoice data. On the computer where the utility is installed, open launch.command on macOS, or InvoiceToFedEx.exe on Windows. The program opens its local interface in your default browser.

If you have just completed authorization, return to the local utility to load your configuration and read invoices. The software must already be installed; this page does not distribute an installer or launch it automatically.

Connection management

  • Connect or reconnect QuickBooks
  • Disconnect QuickBooks

The current utility does not process payments, change QuickBooks financial records, or purchase shipping labels.
