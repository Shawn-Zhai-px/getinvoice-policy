3. What the utility does

The utility retrieves invoice and related customer information from an authorized QuickBooks Online company, lets you review or manually enter recipient and package information, and exports a CSV file for a shipping workflow. The current version does not create shipping labels, purchase shipping, or modify QuickBooks financial records. CSV compatibility depends on the receiving shipping service and the selected import template.
